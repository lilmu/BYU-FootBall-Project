# FootBall Project

**Author:** Ephraim Pan  

---

## Abstract
  
Success in American football is built on the skillful
planning and execution of strategic plays. Instrumental in the
strategic planning process is the annotation and analysis of video
footage. Given the great deal of effort required to manually
annotate such footage, we intend to develop a system that
automates this process, effectively saving time and money for
coaches and athletes. Several similar works have been done in
the past, however there are few that focus on the differentiation
of offense and defense—a crucial step for performing further
analytics and the major focus of this paper. Therefore, we
propose a network architecture that leverages player positions to
train a multi-layer perceptron network to perform this task. We
compared the proposed network with three other experimental
architectures based on their performance on real game data.
Results indicate that the proposed network architecture achieves
up to 98.03% accuracy.
